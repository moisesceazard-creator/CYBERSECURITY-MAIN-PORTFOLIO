Security Onion for Network & Host Visibility and Case Management — MIS Office, Tanauan City Hall

Deployed Security Onion into a live government network that had only a perimeter firewall and relied on external advisories for threat awareness. Built network visibility (Suricata, Zeek via SPAN port), host visibility (Elastic Agent on five pilot endpoints), custom Kibana host and network monitoring dashboards, STRIDE- and ATT&CK-mapped detection rules for brute force, port scanning, USB exfiltration and C2 traffic, plain-language Telegram alerts via ElastAlert 2, and case management with Security Onion Cases, grounded in NIST CSF 2.0 and the Philippine NCSP 2023–2028. Validated against five simulated attack scenarios, then hardened the platform after the defense with MFA, secrets storage and key-only SSH. Formally endorsed by the MIS Department Head for continued use.

I'm a fourth-year BSIT student majoring in Information Security at De La Salle Lipa, focused on Blue Team work: SOC monitoring, SIEM operations, threat detection, vulnerability assessment, and patch management. I learn best by building lab environments, attacking them in a controlled way, and documenting what the detections caught — and what they missed.
